Jesus is coming. We want everyone ready.

As Seventh-day Adventist men, our first mission is to help people know that Jesus is returning, and to be prepared and ready to meet Him. We share that hope with kindness, compassion, and practical support, as men who care in word and in deed.

Peter reminds us that we look forward to a new heaven and a new earth filled with God’s righteousness, and that the Lord’s patience gives people time to be saved. So we live holy lives while we wait, and we make good use of the time.

Two psalms hold us up

David asks the same question twice: who can come near to God? Everything this group does starts with his answers.

Psalm 15:1–5

A man who dwells in God’s presence walks blamelessly, practices righteousness, speaks truth from his heart, keeps his promises even when it hurts, and refuses to exploit the vulnerable.

1 LORD, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill? 2 He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, and speaketh the truth in his heart. 3 He that backbiteth not with his tongue, nor doeth evil to his neighbour, nor taketh up a reproach against his neighbour. 4 In whose eyes a vile person is contemned; but he honoureth them that fear the LORD. He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not. 5 He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.

  1. Walks blamelessly

    His private road and his public road are the same road.

  2. Practices righteousness

    He checks his work against God’s plumb line.

  3. Speaks truth from his heart

    No backbiting, and no reproach against a neighbor.

  4. Keeps his promises

    Even when it hurts, he does not change his word.

  5. Protects the vulnerable

    He refuses to profit from anyone’s weakness.

Psalm 24:3–4

True standing before God requires clean hands and a pure heart: upright outward actions paired with uncorrupted, honest inward motives.

3 Who shall ascend into the hill of the LORD? or who shall stand in his holy place? 4 He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart; who hath not lifted up his soul unto vanity, nor sworn deceitfully.

Clean hands

What men can see: how we work, spend, lead at home, and treat people.

Pure heart

What only God sees: our motives, our thoughts, and why we do what we do.

No man climbs alone

Each month has a rhythm: time in the Word, honest prayer, work in the community, and a day outside with brothers.

  1. Week 1

    Open the Word

    Study one passage together and ask what it requires of us this month.

  2. Week 2

    Pray and confess

    Meet in pairs or threes. Tell the truth about marriage, work, money, and temptation, then pray.

  3. Week 3

    Serve the city

    Put on work gloves and go meet a real need close to home.

  4. Week 4

    Climb together

    Hike Monte Sano, share a meal, and bring a friend or a son.

Changing the world around us

  • Repair homesFix ramps, roofs, and yards for widows, seniors, and single mothers.
  • Feed neighborsCook, pack, and deliver meals to families across Madison County.
  • Mentor young menGive boys without a steady example time, skills, and encouragement.
  • Visit the forgottenSit with men in hospitals, care homes, and jail ministries.
